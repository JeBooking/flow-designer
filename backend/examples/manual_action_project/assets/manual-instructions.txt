纯人工动作演练 / Manual action practice

本示例没有真实设备，不连接任何设备，也不调用 Python。
This example has no real device, connects to no equipment, and calls no Python.

1. 人工检查时，模拟发现问题选“是”，无法确认选“否”进入升级。
   At inspection, choose Yes for a simulated finding, or No to escalate.
2. 在人工处置停点，自行模拟完成操作，例如在纸上记录“模拟处置完成”。
   At the manual action, simulate completion yourself, for example by writing a completion note.
3. 核验模拟结果后选择 OK 才会进入“问题解决”。
   Verify the simulated result and choose OK to continue to Problem Solved.
4. 选择“不 OK”保留同一个人工处置任务，不进入后继。可继续处理后选 OK，或回退检查。
   Not OK keeps the same action pending and does not advance. Continue working and choose OK later, or recheck.
